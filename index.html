How to play
  • There are 36 cards: three copies of each number from 1 to 12. Every hand is sorted from lowest to highest.
  • On your turn, reveal cards one at a time. Each reveal is either any face-down middle card, or the lowest or highest hidden card in any hand, including your own. Cards you can pick float slightly above the others. Your own cards are held up in front of you; point at one to enlarge it.
  • If a card does not match the one before it, your turn ends and every revealed card goes back where it was.
  • Reveal three of the same number and you keep them as a trio. Your turn then ends.
  • Simple mode: win with any three trios. Spicy mode: win with two connected trios, whose numbers add up to 7 or differ by 7 (the small numbers on each card).
  • The trio of 7s wins at once in either mode.
